## Web Redirector and Blocker

**Web Redirector and Blocker** gives you complete control over your web navigation. Whether you want to automatically redirect outdated bookmarks to their new working domains, or simply block annoying chat windows and unwanted websites, this extension has you covered. 

Instead of forcing predefined lists on you, this extension gives you the freedom to build your own custom rules exactly the way you want them.

**Key Features:**
- 🔄 **Custom Redirects:** Easily set up rules to automatically redirect old or broken domains to new addresses (e.g., `old-site.com` → `new-site.com`).
- 🚫 **Site Blocking:** Block any specific domain with a single click to keep distractions and unwanted popups away.
- ⚡ **Lightweight & Fast:** Built on modern Manifest V3 technology, ensuring your rules are applied instantly in the background without slowing down your browser.
- 🔒 **Privacy First:** All your custom rules are stored locally on your device. The extension does not collect, store, or transmit any of your personal data or browsing history.
- 🌍 **Multilingual Support:** Native support for English, Turkish, French, German, and Chinese.

**How to Use:**
1. Click the extension icon to open the popup interface.
2. Choose your action: **Redirect** or **Block**.
3. Enter the target domain (e.g., `example.com`).
4. If redirecting, enter the new destination domain.
5. Click "Add" and the rule is instantly active!
